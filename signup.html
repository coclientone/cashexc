Frequently asked questions

Which currencies will Smart Transfer support?

The production platform can list the currencies enabled through its payment and banking partners. The calculator currently uses demonstration rates.

Can customers transfer to Visa or Mastercard?

Eligible card transfer functionality can be offered where supported by the connected card/payment provider and applicable regulations.

Will customers be able to track transfers?

Yes. The planned account area includes transfer status, history and digital confirmation records.

Are the exchange rates live?

The demonstration calculator is not live. A production version should connect to a reliable exchange-rate provider and clearly display the source and timing basis.
